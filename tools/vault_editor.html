OFR vault editor

Paint a room, watch it check itself, save the .txt into assets/vaults/. Same rules as tests/vault_lint.gd — if it passes here it will load in the game.

Cave vaults (kind: cave): press generate cave for a shape grown by the game's own rules, then paint it, and save it into assets/vaults/caves/. Creatures by name are on the creatures tab. Drafts can wait in tools/vaults_waiting/.

Palette

A named creature is saved as a digit with a place 1: cave bear line in the file. Wild animals cost the room nothing; a monster spends its threat budget, and each appears only where the game could meet it.

Click to paint, drag to fill. Right-click erases back to outside-the-vault.
